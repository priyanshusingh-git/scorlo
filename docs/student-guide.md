# Student Guide

## What Scorlo gives you

Scorlo is your academic workspace for:

- dashboard overview
- semester results
- personal rankings
- profile details
- support issue reporting

## Getting started

1. Open the login page.
2. Create an account with an allowed email address.
3. Verify your email.
4. Log in.
5. Link your academic profile using:
   - roll number
   - date of birth

If the app finds your academic record, your account becomes linked and your student workspace opens.

## Main student sections

### Home

Shows your main academic summary, including:

- CGPA
- overall percentage
- latest SGPA
- active backs
- progress

### Results

Shows your semester archive and summary export actions.

### Rankings

Shows your personal standing across the stored ranking views used by the app.

### Profile

Shows your profile and linked academic record details.

### Support

Use this when:

- something in your academic record is wrong
- a result is missing
- you have a link problem
- your account needs help

You can submit an issue and later review its status and any admin note.

## Password and email actions

- Forgot password sends a reset link if the account exists.
- Email verification is required before sign-in is completed.

## Logout behavior

- Logging out clears the app session.
- If the browser tries to restore an old page from history, the app should push you back to login.

## Important limits

- Students can only access student routes.
- Students cannot access admin tools.
- Your dashboard is built from app snapshots for speed, but access still depends on a valid server session.
